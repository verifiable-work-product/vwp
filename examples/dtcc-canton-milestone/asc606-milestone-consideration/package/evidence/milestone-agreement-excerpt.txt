CANTON COIN MILESTONE PARTICIPATION AGREEMENT — EXCERPT (ILLUSTRATIVE)
Parties: DTCC (Applicant) and the Canton Foundation
Signing date: 2026-01-15

RECITAL
  The Applicant may earn up to 10,000,000 Canton Coin (CC) by achieving the
  milestones set out below. Canton Coin is not transferred at signing. Coin
  associated with each milestone remains UNMINTED and held in the network's
  Unclaimed Rewards pool until the milestone is verified and released.

SECTION 2 — MILESTONES AND ASSOCIATED COIN
  Milestone 1 — Announcement:      publication of a joint press release.        2,000,000 CC
  Milestone 2 — Go-live:           production go-live of the Applicant's
                                   application on Canton.                        6,000,000 CC
  Milestone 3 — Volume threshold:  cumulative on-network settlement reaching
                                   the stated USD volume threshold.             2,000,000 CC
  Total earnable:                                                              10,000,000 CC

SECTION 3 — RELEASE MECHANISM (ON-CHAIN, COMMITTEE-GATED)
  3.1  For each milestone, the Applicant must present proof of successful
       completion to the Tokenomics Working Group and must present a
       calculation for the number of Canton Coin it should earn for meeting
       the requirements of that milestone.
  3.2  The Accountability Committee will confirm whether the Applicant has
       successfully met each milestone.
  3.3  If the Tokenomics Working Group agrees the milestone has been met and
       agrees with the calculation, an announcement is issued and two-thirds
       of Super Validator operators assign the approved portion of the
       Unclaimed Rewards to be minted to the Applicant.
  3.4  Coin is neither owned nor controlled by the Applicant, and remains
       unminted, until the release in Section 3.3 occurs. Unearned amounts are
       subject to removal.
