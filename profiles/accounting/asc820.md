# Extension Sketch: `vwp-accounting-asc820` (Fair Value Measurement)

> **Status:** Experimental stub (v0.1). This is not accounting advice. All example
> data is synthetic. VWP validation is not an audit opinion, legal opinion, accounting
> opinion, or assurance engagement.

A sketch of how fair value measurement support maps onto VWP objects.

## Shape of a fair value package

- **Claims**
  - The asset (or liability) is measured at fair value of X as of the measurement date.
  - The measurement is categorized in the stated fair value hierarchy level.
  - The principal (or most advantageous) market is as identified.
- **Judgments**
  - **Fair value hierarchy level (Level 1 / 2 / 3)** — modeled as an explicit judgment
    with basis (observability of inputs, market activity) and alternatives considered.
    Level 2 and Level 3 categorizations typically warrant `reviewRequired`.
  - Principal market determination; valuation technique selection for Level 2/3.
- **Evidence**
  - **Pricing source evidence**: exchange price extracts, broker quotes, pricing vendor
    files, order book snapshots — with source system, retrieval time, and hash.
  - Market activity support (volumes, quote depth) for level categorization.
- **Calculations**
  - **Valuation tie-outs**: quantity times price equals reported fair value; portfolio
    totals tie to ledger balances; recomputable with declared rounding tolerance.

## Demo validators (illustrative)

- `asc820.price-evidence` — every fair value claim references at least one pricing
  source evidence item covering the measurement date.
- `asc820.level-judgment` — every fair value claim links to a hierarchy level judgment.
- `asc820.tie-out` — fair value calculations recompute from declared quantity and price
  inputs and tie to the claimed amounts.
