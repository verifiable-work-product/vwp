# Extension Sketch: `vwp-accounting-asc842` (Leases)

> **Status:** Experimental stub (v0.1). This is not accounting advice. All example
> data is synthetic. VWP validation is not an audit opinion, legal opinion, accounting
> opinion, or assurance engagement.

A sketch of how lease accounting work products map onto VWP objects.

## Shape of a lease package

- **Claims**
  - The arrangement contains (or does not contain) a lease.
  - The lease is classified as operating or finance.
  - The right-of-use (ROU) asset and lease liability are initially measured at the
    stated amounts.
  - Subsequent measurement (amortization, interest, remeasurement) for the period is as
    scheduled.
- **Judgments**
  - Lease classification (transfer of ownership, purchase options, lease term vs.
    economic life, present value vs. fair value).
  - **Discount rate selection** — rate implicit in the lease vs. incremental borrowing
    rate, with basis and alternatives documented. Typically `riskLevel` medium or
    higher and often `reviewRequired`.
  - Lease term, including renewal and termination options reasonably certain to be
    exercised.
- **Calculations** (recomputable, with declared inputs and outputs)
  - Present value of lease payments (lease liability).
  - ROU asset initial measurement (liability plus/minus prepaids, incentives, initial
    direct costs).
  - Amortization and interest schedules.
- **Evidence**
  - Executed lease agreement, amendments, payment schedules, discount rate support.

## Demo validators (illustrative)

- `asc842.liability-recomputes` — the lease liability equals the present value of the
  declared payment schedule at the declared discount rate.
- `asc842.rou-ties` — ROU asset initial measurement ties to the liability plus declared
  adjustments.
- `asc842.discount-rate-judgment` — every lease package includes an explicit discount
  rate judgment with a stated basis.
