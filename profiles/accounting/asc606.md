# Extension Sketch: `vwp-accounting-asc606` (Revenue Recognition)

> **Status:** Experimental sketch (v0.1). This is not accounting advice. All example
> data is synthetic. VWP validation is not an audit opinion, legal opinion, accounting
> opinion, or assurance engagement.

This extension maps revenue recognition claims to the ASC 606 five-step model and adds
validators for the arithmetic that model implies.

## Claim taxonomy: the five-step model

Each contract (or portfolio) in scope produces claims aligned to the five steps:

| Step | Claim topic | Typical support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Identify the contract | A contract with a customer exists and is enforceable | Executed contract evidence, approval records |
| 2. Identify performance obligations | The distinct performance obligations are P1..Pn | Contract terms as facts; distinctness judgment |
| 3. Determine the transaction price | Transaction price is X, including variable consideration treatment | Contract pricing facts; variable consideration judgment |
| 4. Allocate the transaction price | Price is allocated to obligations per standalone selling prices | SSP analysis calculation; allocation schedule |
| 5. Recognize revenue | Revenue is recognized at point in time / over time per obligation | Recognition method judgment; revenue schedule calculation |

Judgments that commonly require explicit `Judgment` objects: distinctness of promised
goods/services, variable consideration estimates and constraint, standalone selling
price estimation method, and over-time vs. point-in-time recognition.

## Demo validators

Illustrative validators this extension defines (deterministic, recomputable):

- **`asc606.allocation-ties`** — allocated revenue across performance obligations
  equals the transaction price (within a declared rounding tolerance).
- **`asc606.schedule-ties`** — the revenue schedule total for each performance
  obligation equals the consideration allocated to it.
- **`asc606.step-coverage`** — each in-scope contract has claims covering all five
  steps, or a documented rationale for omission.
- **`asc606.judgment-surfacing`** — recognition-method and variable-consideration
  judgments with `reviewRequired` status appear in the validation summary.

### Data contract (implemented in the reference CLI)

The reference CLI implements the first two of these as `asc606.allocation.tieOut`
and `asc606.revenueSchedule.total`, preceded by `asc606.allocation.schemaValid`.
The data contract they consume:

- `calculations/ssp-allocation.json` — a core calculation of type
  `relativeStandaloneSellingPriceAllocation`, validating against
  [`schemas/v0.1/extensions/accounting-asc606/ssp-allocation.schema.json`](../../schemas/v0.1/extensions/accounting-asc606/ssp-allocation.schema.json).
- `calculations/revenue-schedule.csv` — column contract documented in
  [`schemas/v0.1/extensions/accounting-asc606/revenue-schedule.md`](../../schemas/v0.1/extensions/accounting-asc606/revenue-schedule.md).

See [`examples/asc606-revenue-contract`](../../examples/asc606-revenue-contract).

## Synthetic example (fragment)

```json
{
  "id": "claim-alloc-C1042",
  "statement": "Transaction price of 120,000.00 USD for contract C-1042 is allocated 90,000.00 to the license and 30,000.00 to support, based on relative standalone selling prices.",
  "standardRefs": ["ASC 606"],
  "assertions": ["valuationAllocation"],
  "supportingCalculations": ["calc-ssp-alloc-C1042"]
}
```

Passing these validators demonstrates internal arithmetic consistency of the package.
It does not demonstrate that the contract analysis, estimates, or recognition pattern
are appropriate under ASC 606 — those remain professional judgments.
