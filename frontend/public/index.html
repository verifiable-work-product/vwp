    VWP-Bench — the trust-test battery

    The same format, used as an instrument: CPAs and AI models prepare the identical engagement from the identical evidence, and every package faces the same machine battery — grounding, anchoring, recomputation, keyed values, judgment recognition — before blind professional grading. Verifiability is what makes the comparison honest.
