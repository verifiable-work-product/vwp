# Sources — `rev-606-saas`

Per DESIGN.md §3 (synthetic but grounded): document structure and clause
language are adapted from real public filings; every party, amount, and date
is fictional.

- **Structural skeleton:** Regent Education, Inc. Master SaaS Agreement,
  filed as EX-10.3 to Bridgepoint Education, Inc.'s Form 10-Q (2016), SEC
  EDGAR:
  <https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1305323/000130532316000120/bpi2016q210-qxexx103regent.htm>
  Adapted elements: cover-page + Access Terms + Order Form architecture;
  fees/payment clause mechanics (net 30, non-refundability, late interest);
  term tied to Order Form periods with breach-cure termination; annual
  subscription covering a stated End User allowance with per-End-User
  overage invoiced in arrears; elective-termination clause shape (the
  original's 90-day elective termination addendum, adapted into §9.2 with
  the anniversary condition and administrative fee that create this
  challenge's enforceable-term judgment).
- **Answer-key calibration:** FASB ASU 2014-09 illustrative examples
  (<https://storage.fasb.org/ASU%202014-09_Section%20A.pdf>), particularly
  the examples on contract duration/termination penalties, distinct
  services, relative SSP allocation, and contract modifications.

The fact pattern (parties, pricing, dates, the termination-fee economics,
the SSP study values, and both seeded evidence issues) is original to
VWP-Bench. Canary marker embedded in every evidence document:
`VWPB-R606-7C41`.
